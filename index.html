03 / 05Gajah Mada

Gajah Mada is known as the Mahapatih who united the Nusantara, the prime minister of Majapahit who swore the Palapa Oath.

Little is known of his early life. He rose through the royal guard, the Bhayangkara, after protecting King Jayanegara during the 1319 rebellion. In 1334 he vowed he would not taste palapa until the archipelago was brought under one crown.

Capital / Base

Trowulan was the heart of Majapahit

The capital, known as Wilwatikta, stood on the Brantas river plain near present-day Mojokerto. Its ruins include red-brick gates and temples, and a grid of canals that watered the city. From here Gajah Mada planned the campaigns that brought the archipelago under one crown.
